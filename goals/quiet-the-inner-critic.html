Goal · personal

Quiet the inner critic

Turn the self-critical voice from prosecutor into an occasionally useful consultant.

Silence isn't really on offer. What changes is that you hear the commentary as commentary: a mistake at work stays a mistake at work instead of becoming a verdict about you. The critic still shows up; it just loses its seat at the head of the table. The theme this points to holds the biases that feed the voice and the tools built to work with it.
