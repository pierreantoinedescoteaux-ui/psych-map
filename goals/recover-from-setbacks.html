Goal · personal & at work

Recover from setbacks

Come back from a bad stretch with energy rebuilt, not just willpower reapplied.

A bad month ends as a bad month, not a new identity. Energy comes back through contact with things that register as reward, not through waiting for it to return on its own. The burnout map this points to covers why the flatness happens, why willpower is the wrong tool for it, and the approach built for low-energy starts.

    Hedonic adaptation

    Baselines move, which is part of why a recovering stretch doesn't feel like anything is happening at first.

  • →Featureseffect

    Impact Bias

    A loss hits less hard and lasts less long than you picture — the bad stretch will stop hurting sooner than it feels like it will.

  • →Featureseffect

    Negativity bias

    Bad events stick harder, so a rough month can outweigh a genuinely fine six months around it.

  • →Featureseffect

    End-of-history illusion

    Mistaking how you feel this month for who you are now is the exact trap of a bad stretch.

  • →Featureseffect

    Declinism

    The belief that everything's getting worse makes a setback feel like a trend rather than a patch.
