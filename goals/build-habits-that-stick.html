Goal · personal & at work

Build habits that stick

Make the behavior a default that runs on cue instead of a daily decision.

The behavior happens on an ordinary Wednesday without a vote. Gym clothes by the door, same time, same cue, no motivational debate. The route runs through the following-through map: how cues, routines, and pre-decided plans move a behavior from something you decide daily to something that mostly runs itself.

    Present bias

    Right-now keeps outvoting the version of you that has to actually do the habit.

  • →Featureseffect

    Commitment Bias

    Having said it or done it three days running, you keep going to stay consistent with your own past behavior — a large part of why a streak holds.

  • →Featureseffect

    Moral licensing

    A past good deed becomes credit that pays for skipping today: the most common way a stuck habit breaks.

  • →Featureseffect

    Status Quo Bias

    Once the habit is the default, sticking with whatever you're already doing works in your favour.

  • →Featureseffect

    Spacing Effect

    Practice spread across days sticks better than the same amount crammed in — why 'same time most days' beats making up for it at the weekend.
