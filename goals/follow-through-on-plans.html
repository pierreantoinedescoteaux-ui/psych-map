Goal · personal & at work

Follow through on plans

Have the plan you made on Sunday still standing on Thursday.

Sunday's plan survives to Thursday, and not through gritted teeth. The plan itself carries its trigger, its obstacle, and its response, so execution doesn't hinge on how motivated Thursday happens to feel. The map behind this covers the gap between deciding and doing, and the tools built to close it.
