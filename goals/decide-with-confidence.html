Goal · personal & at work

Decide with confidence

Make the call once, having checked it against the usual biases, and let it stay made.

The decision gets made and stays made. Doubt doesn't have to disappear for that. The choice was tested against the usual suspects (anchoring, sunk costs, loss aversion) before committing, so second-guessing has less to work with. The big-decisions map holds the biases worth checking and the working tool for stress-testing a choice.
