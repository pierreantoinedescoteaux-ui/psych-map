Goal · personal

Start things on time

Begin tasks while there's still calendar left, without waiting to feel ready.

The report gets opened on Tuesday when it's due Friday. The dread didn't vanish; starting just stopped depending on feeling ready. The route runs through the procrastination map: why the delay is so predictable, and the tools that decide the when-and-where in advance so starting isn't a fresh negotiation each time.

    Present bias

    Planning fallacy

    The task looked like a shorter job than it is, which is part of why 'I'll start later' felt safe.

    Action Bias

    You'd rather do something — the inbox, the dishes — than sit with the discomfort of the actual task.
