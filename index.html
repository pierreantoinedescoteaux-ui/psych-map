A guide to psychology, drawn as a map.

Psychology, the art of understanding the mind and behaviour, can be overwhelming. This site is aimed to make it easier to navigate.

By sourcing and organizing resources that already exist that will help you think better, navigate your emotions, learn about different psychological theories and therapeutic modalities.

This is a passion project, updated periodically by Pierre-Antoine Descoteaux (read more about me).
