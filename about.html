About

Hi, I’m Pierre‑Antoine.

I’m a builder based in Montreal and Vancouver. Most recently I co-founded Bridge2AI, an AI production-scheduling software company for mid-market manufacturers, where I owned product direction and commercial strategy as COO alongside a technical co-founder. Before that I spent two years at C2 Montréal in sales operations and enablement, and founded The Growth Hub, a pair of coliving houses for entrepreneurs in Vancouver and Montréal.

Psychology is the long-standing interest behind this site. I read it in my spare time and found it hard to know what I don’t know so I’m building a map for myself and others. There’s no AI in the product; the build leaned on it heavily (Claude Code, end to end).

This site is a fun side project, updated periodically — hope you like it, Pierre.
