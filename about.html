About

Hi, I’m Pierre‑Antoine.

I’m a builder based in Montréal. Most recently I co-founded Bridge2AI, an AI production-scheduling software company for mid-market manufacturers, where I owned product direction and commercial strategy as COO alongside a technical co-founder. Before that I spent two years at C2 Montréal in sales operations and enablement, and founded The Growth Hub, a pair of coliving houses for entrepreneurs in Vancouver and Montréal.

Psychology is the long-standing interest behind this site. I read it in my spare time and kept wanting a reference that didn’t assume I already knew the name of what I was looking for, so I built one. There’s no AI in the product; the build leaned on it heavily (Claude Code, end to end).

I work in French and English. The CV has the full picture.
