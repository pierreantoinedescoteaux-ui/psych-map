How and why I’m using external resources/content

My goal is to organize and make awesome psychological resources being discovered by people. I don’t proclaim to be author or the source of any of the information on this site, but want to act more as an intermediary. I would love help your research or organization get known. My approach is to work on user journey and try to make the information as easy to navigate, filter through and apply as possible. I use a mix of product design (UI/UX), information mapping and vulgarization to achieve this.

See more about my work and portfolio here.

Sources
