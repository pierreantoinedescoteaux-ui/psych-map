Social Norms

We shape our behavior around what everyone else seems to be doing, often without deciding to.

Use

Useful to notice when you're doing something because it's actually right for you, or just because it's what's expected around you.

Social norms are the shared, mostly unspoken expectations about how to behave in a given setting — what counts as normal, polite, or acceptable to the people around you. Nobody hands you a rulebook. You absorb it by watching what everyone else does and quietly matching it.

Read the restShow less

Move to a new office and you'll spend the first week reading the room: does everyone eat lunch at their desk, or in the kitchen together? Do people knock before entering, or just walk in? Within days you're doing whatever the room does, usually without ever being told the rule existed.
