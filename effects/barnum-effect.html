Barnum Effect

A description vague enough to fit anyone still feels like it was written just for us.

Use

Useful to read personality quizzes and horoscopes with a more skeptical eye.

The Barnum effect is our tendency to read a vague, generic description and experience it as uncannily specific to us personally. Handed a personality profile — 'you have a need for other people to like you, yet you tend to be critical of yourself' — most people rate it as a highly accurate read of their character. Everyone in the room got the identical paragraph. The description works precisely because it's built to fit almost anyone; the read stays generic while the feeling of being personally seen is entirely real. Horoscopes and cold-reading acts run on the same mechanism.
