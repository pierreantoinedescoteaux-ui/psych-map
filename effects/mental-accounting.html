Mental Accounting

We treat a dollar from a bonus differently than a dollar from our paycheck, even though money doesn't come with labels.

Use

Useful to notice you're spending a windfall more freely than you'd spend the same money earned normally.

Mental accounting is our habit of sorting money into separate mental buckets — rent money, fun money, savings, a windfall — and treating each bucket by different rules, even though every dollar in a bank account is identical. Win two hundred dollars at a casino and it feels like 'the casino's money,' fair game to bet again. Earn the same two hundred dollars at work and it goes straight to the electric bill. The cash is interchangeable. The label we put on it isn't, and the label is what actually drives the spending.
