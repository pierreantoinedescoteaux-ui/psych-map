17:T498,<p>The mechanism is a gap between process and output. The machinery that weighs options and produces a preference runs outside awareness; consciousness receives the verdict. Asked why, we do what we would do for anyone else — generate a plausible theory — except that when the theory is about ourselves, it arrives feeling like memory rather than guesswork. The same asymmetry feeds our self-assessments: we judge others by what they do, and ourselves by the clean-feeling contents of the inner view.</p>
<p>You pick an apartment and cite the light. The light is real, and so were the rent, the commute, the mood you toured it in, and the fact that it was seen last. Introspection cannot rank those forces; it can only tell a story in which the tellable one did the work.</p>
<p>The honest handle is to weight the outward record. What we repeatedly do under pressure is better evidence about our motives than any account we produce of them, and the account deserves the same polite skepticism we would give anyone else&#39;s. How far inward access actually extends is still argued in the literature; the confidence we place in it is the part that reliably overshoots.</p>
