2:T441,<p>The finding traveled well beyond photographs. In a supermarket, shoppers chose between jams, got a manipulated jar, and defended the flavor they had turned down moments earlier. Similar swaps have worked on the taste of tea, on political survey answers, even on eyewitness identifications. The reasons people give for the un-chosen option are delivered with the same confidence and detail as reasons for real choices.</p>
<p>The mechanism seems to be that we keep no rich record of the choosing itself. The decision process leaves a thin trace; what we hold onto is the outcome in hand. When a &quot;why&quot; question arrives, explanation runs forward from that outcome, not backward from the vanished process — so it works just as smoothly on an outcome that was never ours. From inside, constructing a reason and remembering one feel the same.</p>
<p>Which suggests where the handle is. A reason written down at the moment of the decision — even one line — is a record the later story cannot quietly overwrite. Explanations produced afterward deserve to be held as drafts.</p>
