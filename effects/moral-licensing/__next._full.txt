17:T427,<p>The goal being protected is the moral self-image, and the trick is accounting. Judged on its own, skipping the donation or ordering the third drink might register as a small failure. Judged against the week&#39;s balance — the gym sessions, the favor for a friend — it registers as a fair withdrawal. The deed doesn&#39;t change; the ledger it&#39;s posted to does.</p>
<p>You spend Saturday morning helping a friend move, and by evening the recycling goes unsorted with unusual serenity. That&#39;s the plausible everyday scale of the thing — and it&#39;s worth holding loosely, because the lab record is genuinely wobbly. The meta-analytic effect is small, published studies run larger than unpublished ones, and direct replications have struggled. Licensing is best treated as a real but modest tilt, not a law of moral hydraulics.</p>
<p>The countermove is to close the account: let each choice answer &quot;is this what I want to do?&quot; rather than &quot;have I earned it?&quot; The first question has an answer; the second has a negotiator.</p>
