Take-the-best Heuristic

When choosing between options, we often lean on just the one cue that seems most telling and ignore the rest.

Use

Useful to notice when you're deciding on a single standout cue instead of weighing every option in full.

The take-the-best heuristic is a shortcut for choosing between options: instead of weighing every relevant factor, we pick the single cue that seems most likely to matter and let it decide the outcome. Comparing two job offers, someone might settle the whole choice on salary alone, skipping over commute, culture, and growth potential entirely. Researchers who study these fast decision rules find they often perform close to more elaborate weighing methods, especially when time or information is limited — trading thoroughness for speed, and usually getting away with it.
