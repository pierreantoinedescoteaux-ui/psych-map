Theme · personal & at work

Following through

The gap between the plan you made and the person who was supposed to run it.

The plan was good. Sunday-evening you was sure about it: the gym schedule, the side project, the earlier bedtime. Then Tuesday arrived and a different person (same name, less energy) was expected to execute. Researchers call the space between deciding and doing the intention–action gap, and they treat it as a handoff failure between the self that plans and the self that acts, not as a character flaw.

Read the restShow less

The entries here are about closing that gap without leaning on willpower. Present bias explains why the handoff fails: the two selves price the same payoff differently. The ego depletion research, now contested, is a useful case study in why willpower makes a shaky foundation either way. The tools share one strategy: move the decision earlier. Implementation intentions and WOOP pre-decide the when, the where, and the what-if-obstacle. The habit loop moves the behavior from daily vote to default setting.
