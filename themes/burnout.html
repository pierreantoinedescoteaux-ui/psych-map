Theme · personal & at work

Burnout

Effort keeps going out, nothing registers coming back, and rest has stopped working.

How it runs — and where to break it

How it builds

  1. causeSustained demands without real recovery
  2. months, not days→causeEffort keeps going out; reward stops registering
  3. →consequenceExhaustion — rest stops recharging
  4. →consequenceCynicism and disconnection from meaning
  5. rebuild contact with reward→toolBehavioral activation

Tired has a bottom; this doesn't seem to. The weekend doesn't recharge anything, the work that used to mean something now just arrives, and the main expectation about the future is more of the same. Burnout gets described as exhaustion, but the part people report as worse is the flatness: effort keeps going out and nothing registers as reward coming back.

Read the restShow less

The map here is small and deliberate. Hedonic adaptation explains part of the flatness: baselines move, and the wins stopped registering because they became the baseline. The ego depletion literature, contested as it is, frames the useful questions about how depletion actually works. Behavioral activation is the counterintuitive tool: rebuilding contact with rewarding activity in doses small enough to survive low energy, instead of waiting for energy to return on its own. The self-compassion break addresses the self-blame that usually rides on top, adding cost without adding rest.
