A letter · why this exists

I read a lot of psychology in my free time and I kept facing a few challenges about resources out there:

Self help books are full of unproven concepts and marketing slogans with big promises. It’s hard to understand what has been studied, proven or is currently being studied. What books/authors are part of what movements? How do these movements disagree? What tools would help me right now?

It’s quite overwhelming at time. It’s common to read contradictory advice. Does emotions preface actions or the other way around? So the first idea was to create a map of psychology theories and their core fundamental hypothesis. Then show what applied frameworks have been established from these theories.

The goal is that this may help you understand the different movements better when learning psychology or maybe seeking professional help which is already quite difficult in situation of needs.

I started mapping biases because I found extensive resources on the Decision Lab page and just felt that it was hard to navigate practically. So I mapped their database to core questions and areas of focus and added filters to navigate them.

This is my core belief in building this site: the resources already exists they are just hard to navigate.

Hope you find it helpful,

Pierre
